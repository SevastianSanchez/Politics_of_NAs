The scores · download from the index site or Zenodo

vsi_scores published

The Visible States Index (VSI): how much a government reports to the UN SDG database, one score per country-year. Estimated by a two-parameter logistic item response model over 524 series. Each series is treated like a test question and each country-year like a test-taker, so how hard each series is to report is estimated rather than assumed. Scores are posterior means (EAP), so vsi_se is a posterior standard deviation. A 95% interval is roughly the score ± 2 SE.

vsi_goal_scores published

The Goal-level VSI: one reporting score per country-year per SDG goal, so the measure can say what a government reports less of, not only that it reports less. Each goal is a separate model fitted on that goal's own series, drawn from the same 524, so a goal's score owes nothing to the other fifteen. The country's overall reporting level is included. Sixteen goals are scored; goal 7 is not, having only three series, too few to fit. Across the sixteen, a median of 64% of the variation in goal-level reporting is not explained by the overall index.

Held goals are still published in full.
vsi_goal_relative_scores published

The Goal-relative VSI: does a country report more or less on a goal than its own overall level would predict? From one bifactor model, fitted on all 524 series at once: a general factor for overall reporting, plus one factor per goal for what is left. Positive means more than expected, negative less. Same goals as the Goal-level VSI; goal 7 is not scored. New in v1.4.0.

series_variant_map input

Which series repeat another. Some measurements are published twice, for example stunting as a percentage and as a count. Kept, they would let a country earn two points for one act of reporting. A series is collapsed into its cluster only when it has the same reported/not pattern in every country-year and measures the same thing; the 607 → 524 step. Built by build_variant_map.R; every fit halts if it is missing or stale.

series_stability reference

Each series' lifespan, from the data and the UN's update log. The SDG framework grew during 2015–2023, so some series simply did not exist in early years. The 409 series live in every year form the stable core, used to check that the index is not driven by framework growth. Built by build_series_stability.R.

geo_exclusions reference

Every geography that left its own code, and why. The index scores sovereign states; uninhabited places, defunct states, dependent territories and sub-national strata are dropped, and the UK's three devolved jurisdictions are rolled up into the UK. Generated from the same list that drives the filter, so it cannot drift from what happened. 197 geographies remain.
